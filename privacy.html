Introduction

FocusDrive is a focus and simulation app. This policy describes our handling of your information.

Information We Collect

1. Location Data

  • Uses location services to plan routes
  • Requests approximate location during use
  • We don’t share this data; it stays on your device
  • Delete by clearing app data or uninstalling

2. Purchase and Subscription Data

  • We manage subscriptions via RevenueCat
  • RevenueCat may collect:
    • App Store transaction IDs
    • Device identifiers (anonymous)
    • Purchase history
    • See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy
  • Transaction data is processed by Apple’s payment system

3. App Usage Data

  • Storage of settings on your device:
    • Remaining journey uses
    • Subscription status
  • We do not transmit or sync this data

4. Map and Routing Data

  • Uses Apple MapKit for routes and locations
  • Search queries are processed by MapKit
  • See Apple’s Privacy Policy

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide location-based features
  • Verify and manage subscriptions
  • Improve app functionality
  • Uses third-party services (MapKit, RevenueCat)

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not share your location or personal information with advertisers
  • Data remains on your device unless required for core features
  • Third-party service providers (RevenueCat, Apple) may collect data per their policies

Data Storage and Retention

  • Most data stays on your device
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local data
  • Purchase records retained per App Store and RevenueCat requirements
  • We do not maintain a separate user database

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • MapKit: maps and routing
  • App Store: payment processing

Children's Privacy

  • Not intended for users under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13

Changes to This Policy

  • Changes will be posted here
  • Significant changes will be noted in app updates
  • Continued use implies acceptance
