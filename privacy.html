Introduction

FocusDrive is a focus and simulation app. This policy describes our handling of your information.

Information We Collect

1. Location Data

  • Uses location services to plan routes
  • Requests approximate location during use
  • We don’t share this data; it stays on your device
  • Delete by clearing app data or uninstalling

2. Purchase and Subscription Data

  • We manage subscriptions via RevenueCat
  • RevenueCat may collect:
    • App Store transaction IDs
    • Device identifiers (anonymous)
    • Purchase history
    • See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy
  • Transaction data is processed by Apple’s payment system

3. App Usage Data

  • Storage of settings on your device:
    • Remaining journey uses
    • Subscription status
  • We do not transmit or sync this data

4. Map and Routing Data

  • Uses Apple MapKit for routes and locations
  • Search queries are processed by MapKit
  • See Apple's Privacy Policy

5. Weather Data

  • Uses Apple WeatherKit API to display basic weather information
  • Weather data is fetched based on your location
  • Only basic weather data (temperature, conditions) is displayed
  • Location data is sent to WeatherKit only to retrieve weather information
  • Weather data is processed by Apple's WeatherKit service
  • See Apple's Privacy Policy for WeatherKit data handling
  • Weather data sources: WeatherKit Data Sources
  • " Weather" is a trademark of Apple Inc.

6. Analytics Data

  • We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data
  • Collected data includes:
    • Page views and navigation events
    • User interactions and actions within the app
    • Device type and app version
    • General usage patterns (all anonymized)
  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • Data is used solely to improve app functionality and user experience
  • See Firebase's Privacy Policy for more details

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide location-based features
  • Display weather information for your location
  • Verify and manage subscriptions
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Analyze anonymous usage patterns through Firebase Analytics
  • Uses third-party services (MapKit, WeatherKit, RevenueCat, Firebase Analytics)

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not share your location or personal information with advertisers
  • Data remains on your device unless required for core features
  • Anonymous analytics data is sent to Firebase Analytics for service improvement
  • Location data is shared with WeatherKit to retrieve weather information
  • Third-party service providers (RevenueCat, Apple, Firebase) may collect data per their policies

Data Storage and Retention

  • Most data stays on your device
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local data
  • Purchase records retained per App Store and RevenueCat requirements
  • Anonymous analytics data is retained by Firebase Analytics per their data retention policies
  • We do not maintain a separate user database

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • MapKit: maps and routing
  • WeatherKit: weather data and forecasts
  • App Store: payment processing
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage analytics and user behavior tracking

Children's Privacy

  • Not intended for users under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13

Changes to This Policy

  • Changes will be posted here
  • Significant changes will be noted in app updates
  • Continued use implies acceptance
