Optional Website Analytics

This section covers this website. The app disclosures below, including Firebase Analytics, apply separately to the FocusDrive app.

Ahmet Ömer uses Umami at analytics.ahmeto.com to understand basic website pageviews and improve the website. Umami is self-hosted on the developer’s Hetzner server in Germany. Analytics data is retained indefinitely unless manually deleted.

Only after you select “Allow analytics” do we load the tracker and send pageviews. Analytics is optional; declining does not affect access. Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis for website analytics is your consent under Article 6(1)(a).

Pageview data includes the page path and title, referring page, browser language, screen size, and time of the visit. Query strings and fragments are excluded from both page addresses and referrers. The analytics server receives your IP address and browser user agent to process requests; Umami uses these to derive approximate location, browser, operating system, device type, and anonymous session identifiers. We do not configure custom events, user IDs, session recordings, or optional performance or error tracking. Umami’s tracker does not use cookies.

We store your acceptance or rejection in this browser’s local storage under focusdrive.umami-consent.v1, with no automatic expiry, so we can remember your choice across pages and visits. A session-storage blocking flag is used to keep tracking off after declining or an unsuccessful save. These preferences are not sent as analytics data. Clearing browser storage removes your choice. If storage is unavailable, tracking stays blocked; your choice may not survive navigation or a later visit.

Use “Analytics preferences” in any page’s footer to change your choice or withdraw by selecting “Decline”. Withdrawal stops future pageviews, including navigation tracking, and saved changes are shared across open tabs. Withdrawal does not undo processing already performed with your consent or automatically delete earlier analytics records.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and object to processing where that right applies. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing, and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. Contact hi@focusdriveapp.com to exercise your rights. Because we do not assign you a personal user ID for analytics, we may be unable to identify records relating to you.

App Information We Collect

1. Location Data

  • Uses location services to plan routes
  • Requests approximate location during use
  • We don’t share this data; it stays on your device
  • Delete by clearing app data or uninstalling

2. Purchase and Subscription Data

  • We manage subscriptions via RevenueCat
  • RevenueCat may collect:
    • App Store transaction IDs
    • Device identifiers (anonymous)
    • Purchase history
    • See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy
  • Transaction data is processed by Apple’s payment system

3. App Usage Data

  • Storage of settings on your device:
    • Remaining journey uses
    • Subscription status
  • We do not transmit or sync this data

4. Map and Routing Data

  • Uses Apple MapKit for routes and locations
  • Search queries are processed by MapKit
  • See Apple's Privacy Policy

5. Weather Data

  • Uses Apple WeatherKit API to display basic weather information
  • Weather data is fetched based on your location
  • Only basic weather data (temperature, conditions) is displayed
  • Location data is sent to WeatherKit only to retrieve weather information
  • Weather data is processed by Apple's WeatherKit service
  • See Apple's Privacy Policy for WeatherKit data handling
  • Weather data sources: WeatherKit Data Sources
  • " Weather" is a trademark of Apple Inc.

6. Analytics Data

  • We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data
  • Collected data includes:
    • Page views and navigation events
    • User interactions and actions within the app
    • Device type and app version
    • General usage patterns (all anonymized)
  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • Data is used solely to improve app functionality and user experience
  • See Firebase's Privacy Policy for more details

How We Use Your Information
